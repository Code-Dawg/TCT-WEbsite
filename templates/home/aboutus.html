The Origin Story

Brewing Authentic Heritage in Every Cup

Founded with a passion for bringing the iconic Mumbai tapri cutting chai culture to the beautiful city of Pokhara, The Cutting Tea started as a modest dream to serve hot, soul-warming tea made the traditional way.

Every morning, our kitchen awakens to the aroma of freshly crushed ginger, green cardamom pods, cloves, and premium organic CTC leaves simmering in whole milk. From our first outlet in Nadipur to our second location in Simalchaur, we stay true to our roots while offering warm hospitality and artisanal baked treats.
