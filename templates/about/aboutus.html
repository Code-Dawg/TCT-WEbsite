OUR STORY

From a Simple Tapri Dream to Pokhara's Beloved Cafe

The Cutting Tea was established with a clear and passionate mission: to recreate the true essence, aroma, and warmth of authentic Indian tapri chai right in the heart of Pokhara, Nepal.

We select fine high-altitude tea leaves, freshly crushed ginger, and aromatic green cardamom to brew our chai in continuous small batches. Paired with our hot bakery morning treats, crunchy snacks, and signature Cutting Burgers, every order is crafted fresh to brighten your day.

Whether you are catching up with friends at our Nadipur uptown cafe or grabbing a full dinner meal at our Simalchaur downtown outlet, we welcome you with open arms and a hot glass of tea.
